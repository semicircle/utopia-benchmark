**v0 finding: the T0 tier is saturated, for strong and mid-tier models alike.** Claude Opus 5.5 (effort high) and Kimi (`kimi-for-coding`, through its OpenAI-compatible endpoint) both passed every task in every arm: one-shot with no tools across 8 dialects with standard semantics and 8 with false friends, and as agents with the toolchain. They follow the manual rather than habit: in dialects where switch cases fall through they add a break to every case, and in dialects with no operator precedence they parenthesize `(i % 15) == 0`.

So the seven T0 tasks are a smoke test, not a measurement: a renamed lexicon, unfamiliar syntax and a handful of false friends are easy to absorb from a 15 KB manual when the programs are 5 to 20 lines long. To separate models, Utopia needs longer programs where false friends compound (indexing, integer division, evaluation order, list identity), multi-part tasks, and the platform model with seeded VM bugs: T1–T2 next.
